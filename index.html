WHO WE ARE

TRX Luxury Watches is a trusted watch dealer in Dubai with a vast collection of high-end watches from Patek Philippe to Audemars Piguet. These pre-owned watches can be shipped globally. Unlike other watch houses, we specialize in getting you the best deal on some of the most exclusive watches. We also allow our clients to swap their existing premium watches for another one. We are the only company in the UAE offering consignment services on watches around the globe. This allows sellers to get the best price possible for their premium timepieces.

Buying Luxury Watches in Dubai

In order to make things seamless for you, we have a very subtle and direct process of buying luxury pre-owned watches in Dubai. We have many new and used luxury watches in Dubai to choose from. You can find a suitable watch listing and hit the buy button. Upon paying through one of the available methods, you will get the watch delivered or you can choose to pick it up from our store.

Browse Our Collection

TRX Luxury watches has a wide selection of luxury watches available from different luxury brands, including Patek Philippe, Richard Mille and Audemars Piguet, among others. Select Your Watch

Once you have glanced at all the new and pre-owned watches we offer, select the one you want. Take note of the case size, lug-to-lug measurement, and case thickness to determine the best fit for your wrist size.

Make the Payment

That's it! Once we receive confirmation along with the payment, we will ship the watch via our courier partners or send it through one of our drivers. You should get the watch at your doorstep within 72 hours of placing your order.

How Do We Make Sure the Pre-Owned Luxury Watch Is Original?

One of the major problems with buying luxury watches in Dubai is that one can easily fall for a good replica. Unfortunately, the fakes are getting better and hard to spot these days. This is why, we have partnered up with ChronoStreet, Dubai's Leading Luxury Watch Service Center to get all our watches inspected and authenticated by them. Their team consists of highly talented watchmakers with over 30 years of experience working with watches like Rolex, AP, Patek Philippe and more. With the level of experience they have, they can almost immediately tell if there is something wrong with a watch. We only list the watch on our website, once it gets a green signal from the experts.

Here's how the watch experts determine if the watch is 100% genuine and good for our clients.

Examine the Watch Inside Out

Fakes or super fakes have got really close to counterfeiting the original design (for better or for worse). However, one can look closely at a few things to identify if the watch is real or fake. Upon looking at the tiny details, our horologists can determine if the watch is real or fake. Most of these luxury brands infuse a tiny detail to tackle counterfeiting. Besides that, our team also disassembles the watch to check if the internals have never been replaced and are in good functional condition. After a thorough check using their professional-grade equipment, our horologists can determine if the watch is fake or real.

Box and Paperwork

While buying a pre-owned watch, the most important thing is the box and paperwork. This not only helps us determine the watch's originality but also gives us the authenticity of the purchase. If the two things are present, we can be sure that the watch was bought and not stolen.

Service History of the Watch

Just like buying a pre-owned car, we need to go through the service history of the premium watch. This helps us determine whether the watch was well taken care of and serviced in a timely manner. We not only go through the service history but also check the smooth running of the watch ourselves. With this, we can ensure that our customers get the best experience with their newly purchased watches.
